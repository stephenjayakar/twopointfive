Smoke cost — where a plume's milliseconds go, nine measured attempts, two wins, and how far it now scales

Branch perf/smoke-scale off the fire prototype. The maintainer's ask, verbatim: "the main thing to figure out is how to make smoke cheaper while still preserving the current fidelity … see how far it can scale." Method = the ray-traversal lab's: instruments first, a profile, hypotheses ranked by it, serialized-union interleaved A/Bs, exactness proven where claimed, negatives written up. The notebook with every number is docs/internal/smoke-perf.md. M4 Max · Chrome / Metal · high 1368×770 → 1777×1000 unless stated. This GPU was SHARED with other lanes' pages all session, so every A/B is interleaved with a rotating start and filtered: a round counts only if every variant sits within 5 % of its own quietest round, and the quoted number is the minimum over rounds of the round medians.

TL;DR
  • Where it goes. Render-side, a plume costs the direct pass's shadow-ray transmittance and almost nothing else: 0.6 ms for one canister → 1.9 ms for eight (of a 5.8 ms pass); the froxel J (0.13–0.2) and the per-pixel march (0.2) are flat and small; gather, cascades, reflections, particles pay nothing. Nine in ten lit shadow rays in the room cross a plume's 3.5 m BOX and march eight samples — 10–14 M atlas fetches a frame — and with eight boxes live every sample tested all eight. Solver-side: 0.50 ms per busy domain step (pressure 35 %), up to six at full rate at high.
  • Win 1 (exact, on): shadow rays now visit only the domains whose occupied-brick box the segment meets (the solver writes that box behind its occupancy words). Bit-exact against main's function over 50 M random segments; −1.2 ms of direct at eight domains (−20 % of the pass), −0.7 at three, 0 at one.
  • Win 2 (bounded, off by default, recommended 0.5): a domain nothing is emitting into steps every p-th frame, p from how fast its VISIBLE smoke moves, so the plume never moves more than half a voxel between the steps you see: the smoke pass of three hanging plumes 1.25 → 0.79 ms (−37 %; −63 % at 1.0). Active canisters keep their price.
  • Negatives, measured: a per-tile 'can this tile see smoke' gate in restir (+0.1 ms: presence costs nothing to gate), prefiltered mips of the field for shadow rays (±0.06: the fetch was never the cost), a branch-free single-domain sample loop (+0.2 and last-bit different), the froxel cull's smoke gate (nil), kernel fusion / batching (a dispatch costs 1 µs here), fewer pressure pairs (the solver's own soak says no). Fewer transmittance samples is real (−0.3‥0.5 ms at 4) but blotches the plume's shadow: 8 stays, the knob exists.

5. What to port to main, and how (main has no fire prototype; none of this touches it)

  1. T1 — common.wgsl: smokeOcc as array<vec4u, 34>; smokeSubWord / smokeSubLo / smokeSubHi / smokeSegment / smokeDensityLod / smokeTransmittanceLod replacing smokeDensityAt's use inside smokeTransmittance (smokeDensityAt itself stays for the march / self-shadow / probe); vol_common.wgsl: smokeTransIn / smokeTransCoarse become one-liners over smokeTransmittanceLod. smoke.wgsl: renderOccPack's box reduction + the word write. smoke.ts: OCC_BUFFER_BYTES (544) for occBuf; engine.ts: the no-smoke stand-in buffer 544 B. Drop the lab's SMK_* consts, counters, smkGate, smokeTransmittanceRef, and restir.wgsl's / froxel.wgsl's lab hunks (T2 / F1 were negative).
  2. D2 — smoke.ts: SmokeParams.calmCells (+ default), DT_CAP / MAX_PERIOD, Domain.since / owed / period, the period scheduler in encode(), writeSimParams(dt, stepDt) returning per-domain offsets (simParamBuf × 9 copies), bricks.vmaxS readback; smoke.wgsl: dilate's wgDilSpeedS + brick stats word 5. A Tab ▸ Smoke row and a soak invariant (a domain never waits more than 4 frames) on port.
  3. Knob — settings.smokeShadowSteps (frame.volCfg.w bits 2‥5, default 8; smokeShadowLod can go with the mips).
  4. The lab — tools/qa/smoke-lab.js, src/render/shaders/smoke_lab.wgsl, tools/qa/smoke-page.py, this page and the notebook; the counters need the SMK_COUNT build of common + restir's debug-view-12 hunk if kept.

6. Caveats

  • The GPU was shared for the whole session (25–90 % busy while this page idled): absolute unions moved ±1 ms between hours; every delta above is from interleaved rounds filtered to their quiet minimum, and rounds that failed the filter are counted in each table. Two early takes (S1 live, one brick-skip A/B) were visibly contended and are quoted as such.
  • 'before' routes the direct pass, gather / reflection / card hits and the composite's glass ray through main's transmittance; the froxel haze's 5-sample transmittance and the light probe keep the new walk in both columns (their deltas were nil), and the solver writes its box words in both.
  • Not measured: ultra / low tiers, the breach's daylight sun through a plume, the QRF stress minute's eight FINE gun-smoke domains (small boxes: T1's mask matters less, the calm rule more), the CPU.
  • The fires' remaining +2 ms of direct per fire is their two point lights inside cluttered pods — a light-count question (one light per fire halves it), parked for the fire lane.
