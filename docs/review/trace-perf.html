EXP: ray traversal — profile, twelve measured attempts, one win

exp/trace-perf off main @ e3d532b · M4 Max · Chrome 148 / Metal 3 · every number from tools/qa/trace-lab.js (serialized frames, interleaved, medians; micro-bench = the lab's own timestamped kernel over the real g-buffer's receivers) · notebook: docs/internal/trace-perf.md

  • Where the time goes (B1 cubicles, high): the tracer is 39 % of the GPU frame — 3.3 ms of any-hit shadow rays (direct 1.3, gather 1.1, cascades 0.65, froxels 0.13) + 1.25 ms of closest hits (gather 0.72, cascades 0.2). The gather spends 87 % of itself inside the tracer.
  • The walk is divergence-bound, not work-bound: a shadow ray loads ~1.6 cell headers, ~7.6 item words, slab-tests ~3.8 boxes; that is ~2 % of ALU peak. The same rays traced with all 32 lanes of a SIMD group agreeing run 4–5× faster; merely letting a group aim at the same light gives 3.1×. Every per-ray micro-optimisation therefore measured inside the noise (V1 unroll, V2 storage bounds, V8 sorted lists) or negative (V3 AABB pre-reject −5 %, V5 flattened loop −11 %, H1 blocker guesses +13 % on the direct pass).
  • The win is on the consumer side: E8, tile-coherent STRATIFIED gather directions (the k-th gather ray of every texel drawn from stratum (k + frame) mod 4, own jitter inside): gather −13…−16 % in every scene at high (2.10 → 1.77 ms B1, 2.56 → 2.16 B2), −12…−14 % at ultra (−0.8 ms), −10 % in the stress minute; frame −2…−4 %; energy identical, temporal noise equal or lower (it is stratified sampling). Not hash-identical by nature (a different sample pattern), so it ships as settings.gatherStrata (default off, ?gatherStrata, __gs.on) for the maintainer to look at and flip.
  • One more lever, priced but not pulled (E11): crediting an off-screen gather hit's direct light without its shadow ray when the whole sum is under k × the pixel's cached irradiance takes another −8 % (k = 0.5 %) to −19 % (k = 2 %) off the gather with no measurable change in the denoised indirect here — but it is a bias by construction, so it is left as a folded-away const for the maintainer.
  • Exact traversal variants worth keeping as hooks, not wins: V6 (characters registered in the trace grid, tested only where a ray's cells carry their bit — exact for occluded / traceClosest, 0 % in these frames because a man stands in every view, but the right seam for the SDF-characters lane) and V4 (occludedT men-last: boolean-exact, hint-equivalent, +6–12 % on incoherent shadow rays, 0 % in the direct pass whose rays are already coherent).
  • Default of the branch = the reference walk (TRACE_VARIANT 0, gatherStrata off): B1 / B3 identity hashes ae9c56db / 674bd13e reproduce boot after boot through the new dispatchers; every 'exact' variant is 0 disagreements over 44 M rays × 12 query/class combos.

The reference walk is already lean: half the item words are rejected by the 1.5 cm height cull before the 32-byte box fetch, five globals are rejected against the segment's bounds, straight-up rays are answered by the ceiling global in one slab test (the 'column path' of H4 has nothing left to win indoors), and every miss pays the character loop (six bounding spheres). ~450 ALU ops and ~165 bytes per shadow ray at 600–900 Mrays/s is a few percent of what the GPU can issue — the cost is lanes waiting for each other.

Identical work per lane runs 4–5× faster: execution and memory divergence between the lanes of a SIMD group is the limiter. The direct pass's real rays already run near 'lightC' (its tiles mostly aim at one light: ≈ 3000 Mrays/s effective from the pass timing); the gather's rays — scattered secondary origins, iid cosine directions — run at the incoherent figure. That is why the only lever that moved a pass was making the gather's lanes agree on a direction stratum (E8), and why trimming instructions off an already-waiting lane did nothing.

5 · The win in detail — E8, tile-coherent stratified gather directions

Why it works: the gather traces 4 cosine rays per half-res texel. Neighbouring texels' k-th rays used to point anywhere in the hemisphere, so the 32 lanes of a SIMD group walked 32 unrelated cell sequences. Drawing ray k of EVERY texel from the same stratum of the hemisphere — (azimuth half) × (cos²θ half), rotated by the frame index — with each texel's own two random numbers as the jitter inside it keeps every ray cosine-distributed and every texel covering all four strata every frame (a stratified estimator: same expectation, never higher variance than iid), draws the same COUNT of random numbers (so everything downstream is untouched), and puts a whole tile's k-th rays into one quarter of direction space. The lab priced exactly that regrouping at 1.25× on gather rays; the pass came in at −13…−16 %.

Where: fgather.wgsl cosineDirK(N, k, K) replacing cosineDir(N) at the two ray-drawing sites (main's RAYS loop; mainGi's K fresh candidates as K azimuth sectors — RIS weights stand, each candidate is still cosine-distributed). passes.ts compiles the module twice behind const GATHER_STRATA (pFG/pFGR and pFGs/pFGRs, all async boot pipelines) and the encode picks by gatherStrata, mirrored from settings.gatherStrata — so it A/Bs live. 6 · The one lossy row — the men-last occludedT (V4 / V6)

V4 (and V6's occludedT, which takes V4's order) never changes WHETHER a shadow ray is blocked — only, when a man stands on the segment AND a box registered in an earlier cell is hit beyond him while a nearer box sits in a later cell, WHICH blocker's distance feeds the PCSS penumbra hint (V0: the box; V4: the man). occludedT's own contract calls that distance 'roughly where … boxes in DDA order ≈ near-first, not nearest', so both are in spec; the pixel difference can only appear in a character's shadow. 8 · What did not work, and why (as valuable as the win)

  • Latency hiding (V1) — pairing the item-word and box-record loads changed nothing: occupancy already hides load latency; the lanes are waiting on each other, not on memory.
  • Uniform vs storage for loop-indexed reads (V2) — no difference for six vec4s; the reach-mask lane's earlier finding (storage cheaper) was about a 17 KB array, not this.
  • ALU trimming on the yawed slab path (V3) — an extra conservative test on the per-item critical path costs every item and rejects few (diagonal partitions have fat AABBs): −5 %.
  • Loop flattening (V5) — the textbook divergence fix made it 6–11 % worse: the flattened body carries both halves every iteration and the nested while-while already lets a SIMD group's lanes share cell steps; Apple's scheduler evidently handles the nested exits well.
  • Blocker caching (H1) — a receiver in shadow can skip its later rays' walks with one slab test, but the on-screen majority is LIT, a lit ray can never be shortcut (proving 'clear' needs the whole walk), and the guess test + the extra live state cost every ray: +13 % on the direct pass. The temporal (last-frame) version has the same cost structure and was not built.
  • Sorted cell lists (V8) — with ~2 boxes slab-tested per loaded cell there is no early-exit order left to exploit: 0.0 %.
  • Sharing the light pick at gather hits (E10) — 0.0 %: after E8 the hits of a tile's k-th rays already cluster, and the off-screen shadow rays are not pick-divergence-bound.
  • Column / short-ray special cases (H4), glass / reach bounds (H7) — the profile removed the premise: vertical rays cost one slab test, glass is not in the tracer, shadow rays already stop at the light.

9 · Structural proposals

  • Ray binning inside the gather workgroup (the general form of E8): generate the tile's 256 rays, sort by direction cell in workgroup memory, trace in sorted order, scatter results back. The lab prices full 32-cell binning at 1.35–1.76× on gather rays vs E8's 1.25×, i.e. maybe another −0.15…−0.3 ms at high, ~1 ms at ultra — but it needs the shading rng decoupled from the direction rng (today ray k+1's direction depends on ray k's shading draws) and a two-phase kernel; a day's work, and a different noise realisation to re-validate.
  • Skip negligible shadow rays at gather hits: directAtPoint spends one shadow ray per off-screen hit however dim its unshadowed light is against the pixel's cached irradiance (the firefly bound's Eref is already computed). Crediting hits below ~1 % of Eref unshadowed is a bounded bias for a share of the gather's 1.1 ms of any-hit rays — a sampling-policy call for the maintainer, half a day with the noise harness.
  • The same medicine for the direct pass, smaller dose: its shadow rays already run near the 'same light per SIMD group' figure (≈ 3000 vs 4157 Mrays/s for identical rays), so sorting a tile's queued rays by light slot in workgroup memory before tracing can buy at most ~25 % of its 1.3 ms any-hit share (≈ 0.3 ms at high) — worth doing only together with the gather's binning, since it needs the same trace-then-scatter-back structure.
  • A per-cell 'character present' bit is the right shape for whatever replaces capsules (V6): the SDF lane should register rigs into region C and march only flagged rays; with mesh shadows at ultra the same mask gates the per-character BVH walks.
  • Not a BVH. With 1079 mostly axis-aligned boxes in 35 KB, a 2D grid + height cull that slab-tests 3.8 boxes per shadow ray, and the limiter being lane divergence rather than node count, a BVH2/4 would trade the grid's O(1) empty-space skipping and stackless walk for similar per-ray test counts and the same divergence. I would not spend the week.
