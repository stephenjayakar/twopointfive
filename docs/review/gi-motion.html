GI in motion — what the ‘noise in motion’ actually is, and every lever measured

Lane v3/gi-motion · 2026-08-11 · docs/internal/gi-motion.md has every number and how it was taken · M4 Max, Chrome / Metal · high = 1368×770 internal → 1777×1000 output, film grain off, the flickering tube held steady, unless a row says otherwise · levels = 8-bit output luma; ‘flow’ = the temporal sd of a world point followed through the pan

Verdict. On both shots of the recording the GI is not the term that moves. Removing the GI term outright (indirect scale 0, everything else identical) leaves the composite's flow under the pan where it was in every dark tile (GI share 0–1 % of the variance in the five clean tiles; 8 % on the panel-lit floor), and the built variants that act on the GI — B (GI denoised at internal res), C (young-history gather rays), D, and A's extra resolution — change no number past the take-to-take spread and nothing I can see in the crops. What moves is the direct chain under camera motion: switching ReSTIR back to the old leader / tail estimator takes 33–56 % of the flow out of the dark tiles and turns its 2–7-px blotches into ½-px grain, and switching off the resolve's motion terms (motion α 0.4, RCAS motion sharpen 0.3) another 10–20 %; the two together (‘floor’) land on the OLD renderer's numbers tile for tile (unlit floor 0.235 vs old 0.244 vs main 0.439 levels) — that pair IS the whole ‘v3 is noisier in motion than the old renderer’ in these tiles, while at rest v3 with the leader path is 20–40× calmer than either. On the static breach shot the wall's slow swim is ReSTIR's crawl too (GI 0 %), and there ReSTIR is the BETTER estimator (the leader path swims 2× more on the sun-and-sky wall).

What I recommend, in this lane's remit (quality.ts rows / settings — an OPT-IN bundle for the maintainer's eye, not shipped as defaults): MOTION_CALM = ReSTIR's spatial reuse everywhere + the extra penumbra ray, and the resolve's motion terms halved (α 0.4 → 0.25, motion sharpen 0.3 → 0.15) — ?motioncalm, Tab ▸ Frame, __gimo.calm(): −14 … −24 % of the pan flow per clean tile at high, the STILL tiles −23 … −51 % (spatial reuse halves ReSTIR's static crawl too), the daylight wall +7 %, +0.79 ms union. The rest is not a row: reservoir reprojection that does not scramble lineages under sub-pixel camera motion, in restir.wgsl — another lane's file; §8 words it for that lane. Ship A (the climb) as the ladder feature it is (it works, and the same commit fixes ‘auto ↑’ on 60 Hz displays, where it never could fire); leave B / C / D in as identity-proven OFF rows that cost nothing off — no tier places them: on these shots they buy nothing at +0.85 / +0.65 ms.

1. The two shots of the recording, reproduced

Both scenes boot ?paused, reset the clock, seed Math.random, zero every anim.phase, park the guards dark, switch the storm and the film grain off and hold the fluorescent tubes steady at the start of EVERY take (a take renders the same jitter / dice sequence whatever ran before it in the page; the tube's occasional 14-Hz dropout bursts had put a whole-tile 16-level pumping into whichever early takes overlapped one). 24 consecutive OUTPUT frames are read back losslessly; each tile is a world rectangle sampled ≈ 1 output px apart and RE-PROJECTED every frame through that frame's own view-projection, so a point of floor is followed through the pan (bilinear luma). S1's follow camera pans 2.44 output px per frame at 1777 wide.

Per tile: flow = temporal sd of luma per world sample, mean over the tile (levels; in brackets ÷ the tile's mean); |Δ| = mean frame-to-frame |difference| (≈ 1.13 × sd for white noise, ≪ sd for a slow crawl); boil = mean of (max − min) over the 24 frames; blotch size = autocorrelation half-width of each frame's residual along the tile's rows, output px. Repeats of main in one page agree to ±2 % (unlit), ±3 % (litFloor), ±8 % (stripe, a thin bright band); the ‘STILL’ column is the same tile with the camera pinned and Sam out of frame. Views: 0 = the composite; D = the composite with the GI term removed (indirect scale 0 — resolve, sharp direct, everything else identical: the GI's marginal share = 1 − varD/var0); 4 / 3 = the indirect-only / direct-only debug views (the terms naked — no resolve, no sharp direct — for attribution of the terms, not of the picture). Two more tiles placed inside the moonlit bands (‘stripe’, ‘moonStripe’) turned out to be occluded by the desk cluster's partition for part of the pan — the sampler follows the floor point with no depth test — and are out of every table; ‘lightEdge’, the boundary of the window light on the dark floor (a penumbra, the largest relative flow of all: 2.2 %), stands in for ‘the stripes’.

Why blotches pointed at the GI and were not: ReSTIR's per-pixel reservoir error is temporally persistent (M-capped history) and the shadow client's à-trous smears it 3–4 px; at rest that is a frozen faint pattern (the documented slow crawl — the STILL column: 0.19 levels vs the leader path's 0.005), under a pan the integer-pixel reservoir reprojection hands ~44 % of pixels a neighbour's reservoir every frame, lineages merge and split, and the frozen pattern becomes one that changes frame to frame at every world point — flow, at blotch scale, strongest where many dim lights compete for two reservoir slots (the ‘unlit’ floor is lit by a dozen distant panels). The leader path keeps no per-pixel state but a bilinear-reprojected ratio history: its error is white per frame and the resolve eats it. On the daylight wall (one sun + sky) the situation inverts: ReSTIR's reservoirs hold the sun and the leader path is the one that swims (S2 wall 1.84 vs 0.905).
