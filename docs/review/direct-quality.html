What it was. A CARRIED light moves every frame, and the reservoirs are a history: under a sweeping torch the temporal weight every pixel inherits describes the cone where it stood a frame ago — the pool's fully-lit interior comes out mottled and crawling, its edge band is one or two fresh rays a pixel, and the shadow denoiser (rightly) drops its own history there too. The muzzle flash never had a history: one shadow ray per pixel on the only frames it exists. What ships. A HYBRID estimator: every light a character carries (owner byte ≠ 0 — torches, Sam's gun light, flashes, tracer heads) bypasses the reservoirs and gets the old leader treatment every frame at the pixels it reaches — K stratified emitter samples per leader (ultra 8 · high 4 · medium 3 · low 2 · verylow 2), shaded exactly from the pixel's own point; static lights keep ReSTIR untouched. Plus the false colour ReSTIR painted along shadow boundaries under two lights of different colour (the "yellow / blue fringes": its belief hue coloured a blocked light's penumbra with a far light the tile still believed — a HUE SPLIT fixes it, 6 663 green px → 0), and two hygiene fixes the sliver hypothesis produced (real, but 0.01 % of a frame): the sharp path no longer borrows a ratio across surfaces, GBAA no longer blends toward a nearer third surface. Every switch off = main byte for byte (48b3212e / b4bceada at high, 2ce6cb9d at medium, on both trees).

The mechanism, and why the fix is structural

  • The reservoir's weight is a history of the class total. The temporal reservoir a pixel inherits carries last frame's estimate of Σ E·V at the texel it came from, re-weighted only by ITS OWN light's change at this point. Under a sweeping cone every pixel's history was made where the cone stood a frame ago: even with every shadow ray clear, the interior of the pool reads a spatially varying, slowly correcting estimate — the mottle. No ray count fixes that; only not reusing does.
  • The confidence cut and the light-motion cue both fire where the cone's falloff crosses a pixel: the history goes (correctly — it is wrong), the pool's edge band is shaded from one or two fresh rays, and the shadow client shows them nearly raw.
  • Thin faces (a jamb's 4-cm reveal beside the leaf's 3-cm slot): the reservoir domain is the UNJITTERED pixel centre, which for a face thinner than a pixel plus its jitter lies on the next surface — its rays answered for the wrong face. Carried lights now trace from the raster's own point.
  • The old leader path traced 8 stratified rays to the top light from the pixel's own point every frame and had no history to be wrong — which is exactly what the hybrid gives every carried light, and only them: fixtures, panels, lamps, the vending fronts and the moon (owner 0, never move) keep the reservoirs, whose calm-frame cost and noise are why they exist.

4 · The sliver hypothesis — measured, refuted as the cause, kept as hygiene

The lead's attribution for the leaks / rims / fringes was the sharp path's ratio fallback: an output pixel none of whose internal texels lies on its surface borrowed the nearest texel's ratio, lit or not. The census (sharp_direct.wgsl now writes its fallback code per output pixel into sharpE's alpha; __sharp.census()) over a 77-view survey of the floor at the follow camera:

survey: no rows

0.008–0.015 % of a frame are slivers and 47–70 pixels a frame are leaks by construction — scattered single pixels on chair legs, reveals and limb edges, not lines and rims. Rule 1 fixes them anyway (one deterministic output-resolution ray per class to the strongest live emitter, the remainder on the neighbours' luminance ratio, both classes alike, ≤ 24 rays a tile), for +0.13 ms of occupancy at high (the traced kernel variant) and no measurable ray time.

5 · The other four notes — evidence, and whose term each is

"Glowing at contact points" = no self-shadow on characters

Direct-only view, a guard against the corridor wall under its panel (capsule proxies; mesh shadows read the same): his boots shine at their unshadowed value INSIDE the shadow his own legs cast round them — every shadow ray leaving a character's pixel skips that character's own proxies (the owner byte; capsules or mesh alike), so what his body shades from the panel above never darkens. Estimator-independent; it is the character-visibility term the SDF-characters lane is building (a soft self-shadow factor). Not touched here.

"Yellow / blue fringes along shadow boundaries" — physical bands, PLUS a false colour ReSTIR painted on them (fixed)

Two lights of different colour put one object's shadow in two places: the band only the warm one cannot reach is lit blue, the band only the cool one cannot reach yellow — the old renderer draws the same bands (left). On top of them ReSTIR's shade step painted a false hue: it colours the class's estimated visible LUMINANCE with the hue of the light mix the pixel BELIEVES visible, and deep inside one light's shadow the believed set can be a far dim light of another colour that still has reach there — in this office the green exit signs: the blue lamp's penumbra came out green. Attribution by A/B in the direct-only view (strongly-green pixels in the shadow tile): ReSTIR on 6 663 (v3 denoiser) / 4 713 (legacy) · ReSTIR off 0 · ReSTIR with the per-sample hue A/B 0 · old 0. The HUE SPLIT (settings.restirHueSplit): the believed mix colours only the luminance its own lights carried; a sample of a light the pixel does not believe visible keeps its own hue → 0. Where every light is believed (the vending floor the belief hue was built for) nothing changes; off = byte-identical.

"Light leaking in door frame"

Not reproduced as a leak in any framing tried (storage / manager / break-room doors at 21 m and 9 m, corridor lit and dark, the leaf widened to shut its slots): with the corridor group off the frame is black through the sharp path and without it (docs/shots dq_darkC*). What every door does show under light is §1's dashed slot / reveal — geometry aliasing the old renderer shares — and, under a MOVING carried light through the reservoirs, thin faces shaded from the wrong surface's point (fixed by the hybrid's ray origin). If the line the maintainer saw is neither, a screenshot's framing would pin it in one capture with the census paint on.

"Red wall leaking on Sam"

Not the direct term (a ratio of irradiances carries no albedo). The composite's joint-bilateral INDIRECT upsample falls back to the nearest gather texel raw when no tap passes its depth / normal weights, and on a limb thinner than a gather texel (3 internal px at high's third-res gather) that texel holds the red partition's bounce — present in the internal path too. The gi lane's diffuse client owns that upsample; noted there, not built here (the maintainer's read of the indirect otherwise: "looking very good").

"Noise in the flashing light" (the tube)

The strobing tube is a STATIC light whose power jumps; the hybrid leaves it to the reservoirs. A jump-frame stratum for it (N stratified rays for a light on the frame its power jumped, its reservoirs carried over frozen) was built and measured wrong in LEVEL: the reservoirs holding other lights still carry the tube's old contribution in their weight (the class total lives in W, not per light), so the tube's own N-ray term counted it twice — the raw jump frame read +53 %, +21 % after taking it out of spatial reuse. Unfixable without per-light weights; dropped, tjank's numbers stand (its per-level noise 1–3× the reference is jump dynamics, iq §3 D).

6 · GBAA's same-surface clamp

An edge pixel blends toward the neighbour across its edge by the far side's coverage — right when that neighbour shows the far side (the background of a silhouette, the other face of a crease), wrong when a NEARER third surface stands over the edge (a man before a partition's top edge lent half his colour to every edge pixel his outline crossed). The clamp: blend only toward a neighbour at or behind the pixel (depth, the take-up rule's tolerance). __iq.crawl V3 / V1 unchanged to the third decimal (the partition tops have nothing nearer over them).
