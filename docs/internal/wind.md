# Wind — the building's air as one field (lane `feat/wind`, 2026-08-11)

> **08-11: the dust MOTES described below were removed** (the maintainer: 'they look weird … it's obvious that they spawn when your flashlight shows up somewhere, not ambiently always present'). The moths stay (`game/moths.ts`). The mote paragraphs are kept as the lane's record only.

The maintainer, verbatim: *"for hvac vents, that should model wind which then also influences the smoke solver"*, with *"dust motes, sodium light
moths, fixture deaths"* approved as the lane's extras. Their two rules for it: ONE MODEL (the field that moves the particles must be the field
that forces the smoke — the guards' smoke sight lines then follow for free) and BOUNDED per-frame work.

## What to try first

- `?quality=high`, Tab ▸ Weather ▸ **wind** rows. Throw a smoke can (3) so it stops under the middle aisle's north end (the diffuser `vent_aisle`
  is over (19.8, 14.6)) and watch the plume: it is held to the floor and driven out along it instead of standing up to the slab. 'wind: master' to 0
  and throw another: the old column. `__wind.at([19.8, 1.5, 14.6])` reads the air there (≈ 1.85 m/s straight down a metre under the face).
- Rake the red cluster's north-west desk (papers at 15.4 / 14.5, under `vent_cub_nw`) with the FN2000 so the stacks go up, let the leaves settle,
  then wait: they tremble on the desk and every few seconds a gust picks one or two up and slides them off. 'wind: vent speed ×' 1.75 makes it a
  draughty office; 'wind: paper wake speed' is when a leaf may go.
- Open the fire exit in the storm (Weather ▸ wind x −3) with a can popped a metre inside it: the plume leans into the room.
- Trip the breaker (or shoot it): every diffuser spins down over four seconds — the plumes straighten — and comes back with the restrike.
- Motes: any guard's torch, or L for the rail light, near the camera — the beam carries drifting dust that is simply not there outside it. Moths:
  the east sodium lamp by the fire exit at night (a dozen at most across the lit lamps; they scatter in a gale / by day / when the lamp is shot).
- Fixture deaths: shoot a lit corridor panel (glass + phosphor shower under the flicker-out), shoot the breaker (the arc-over), kick a locked door
  (the keep's splinters go in with the leaf).

## Design as built

| piece | how |
|---|---|
| **the field** (`game/wind.ts`, `render/shaders/wind_field.wgsl`) | `windAt(p)` = Σ sources, each analytic: a **JET** (a diffuser at `pos` blowing along unit `dir` at `speed` m/s at its face) = straight streamlines out of the cone's virtual apex (r0 = 0.4 × the face's size, half-angle `spread`), speed × 1/(1 + (d/reach)²) down the axis × e^(−(r/σ)²) across (σ = r0 + d·tanSpread), faded to nothing over the last 30 % of dMax = 2.5 × reach and exactly nothing outside 2.6 σ / behind the face / past dMax — a BOUNDED support, so a solver cell or a leaf outside it does nothing at all; a jet blown DOWN at a floor turns to run OUT along it below `deflectH` 0.55 m over that floor, losing 35 % of its speed at the floor (the impinging jet's wall jet: what slides a leaf under a diffuser rather than pinning it — a resting particle hands its OWN surface as the floor, so the desk deflects it too). The **STORM** = the weather's wind vector everywhere outdoors (outside `INTERIOR` / `ANNEX`, under 8 m), easing in over 1.5 m off the facade, 0.56 of it along the ground (10-14, `WX_OWN.wind`: on a level of three or four roofs of its own the storm is kept out ROOF BY ROOF — `windStormRoofs` / `stormVelRoofs`, the lee off the nearest roof — where the two-rectangle record took their bounding box; docs/agents/weather.md 'One wind field outdoors'). A **DOOR GUST** = a jet from just outside each open exterior door along its inward normal, ∝ the leaf's open fraction × (0.35 + 0.65 × how square the wind stands to that face). wind_field.wgsl is the ONE text both GPU consumers compile (particles.ts prepends it to the particle kernels, smoke.ts to the solver's prelude); `jetVel` / `stormVel` repeat it statement for statement on the CPU (the moths, the panel, the probes; `wind-field` compares the numbers and the constants). |
| **the sources** (`Wind` on the Game, built after the doors, `update(dt)` after the weather) | Fixed at construction: one per `level.vents` entry, one storm, one per exterior door (the office: 6 + 1 + 3). Each frame every source folds its state into ONE speed: a vent = authored × `WIND.ventK` × its spin `on` (eased toward 0 / 1 over `rampDown` 4 s / `rampUp` 3 s by its `period`/`duty`/`phase` cycle and by the MAINS — `g.blackout.active` spins every `mains` vent down) × a gust (1 ± `gustAmp` 0.25 on a value noise of the game clock keyed by the source's name hash — deterministic, nobody's dice; the storm's ± 0.35) × `tune.wind`; the storm = |weather.wind| × `stormK` while the weather is on, it is night and it rains or storms; a door = |wind| × open × facing. `active` = the ones over 0.005 m/s. `tune.wind` 0 → both consumers are handed EMPTY lists and update returns before evaluating anything. |
| **→ the particles** (`render/particles.ts setWind`, `particles_common.wgsl`, `particles.wgsl windAir`) | The jets whose support comes within `nearM` 14 m of the view's look-at point (nearest first) + the storm, ≤ `WIND_GPU_MAX` 8, are packed as `ImpulseRec` records read another way, BEHIND the frame's impulses in the same sixteen slots — impulses first, so a bang is never short of a slot (12 impulses leave 4 winds, 16 leave none) — the count in `flags` bits 16‥20, one tail upload for both, nothing uploaded with nothing live. A jet record: pos + reach · axis + speed · (tanSpread, r0, dMax) · floorY · kinds · iflags 2; the storm: the interior rect in pos + radius, the annex in eye + up, the wind in bias × strength, iflags 6; every record also carries the four live knobs as two f16 pairs (seed = wake / kick, spare = debrisK / meander). The update kernel sums the AIR VELOCITY at the particle (`windAir`, records naming its kind only; a resting particle passes its own surface height for the wall-jet turn) and takes drag AGAINST THE MOVING AIR: dust `v = vAir + (v − vAir)·e^(−k·dt)`, chips the same toward `debrisK` 0.15 of it, brass / sparks none; a SHEET's whole aerodynamics (`sheetAir`) run on the relative wind v − vAir and add the air back — the impulse lane's 'co-moving gust' made general (vAir 0 = the equations term for term as they were). A RESTING leaf in moving air TREMBLES (± 0.35 rad per m/s of yaw about its normal at its own 1‥2 Hz — zero-mean, and only the yaw: nothing an uneven dt leaves behind can tilt it off what it lies on) and once every `WIND_WAKE_WINDOW` 0.75 s a wake draw picks up a hashed share of the leaves the air moves faster than `sheetWake` 0.35 m/s over (the share rising to `WIND_WAKE_PMAX` 0.4 as the speed doubles past it): woken, kicked at `sheetKick` 2 × that speed along the air with a 0.45 lift, tipped off flat and set turning by the impulses' own shape response (`sheetKickShape`, shared) — it hops, slides, settles. Counter `hdr[5]` = leaves the wind picked up (`stats.windWoken`). |
| **→ the smoke solver** (`smoke/types.ts setWind`, `smoke.ts writeWind`, `smoke/wind_force.wgsl`, one line in `smoke.wgsl forces`) | The solver is handed EVERY active source and culls per domain itself: for each live domain, the sources whose bound (a sphere round the cone's support; the storm = any domain not wholly inside the interior footprint) overlap the domain's box → a bit in that domain's mask; the first ≤ 8 sources over any live domain are packed into ONE 560-B uniform (`WindBlock`: count · relax · minW · 8 mask words · 8 × `WindSrc`) at group 0 binding 2, uploaded only on frames that carry a source over a live domain (+ once, count 0, after the last such). The `forces` kernel, after buoyancy / confinement / turbulence / the emitters and before its speed clamp, calls `windForce(u, worldP, domain, dt)`: mask 0 = one uniform load and a whole-workgroup branch; else per overlapping source `v = mix(v, v_air, clamp(w · relax · dt))`, w = |v_air| ÷ the source's face speed (1 at a jet's face, falling off with its own profile — a plume rising BESIDE a vent keeps its own motion), relax = `WIND.smokeRelax` 3 /s. The pressure projection then does what air does with it: a down-jet spreads along the floor, a jet into a corner vents the way it can, mass is conserved. Nothing here injects density or heat and **no source ever places, keeps alive or retires a domain** (`writeWind` only reads the live list; `placeDomain` / `lastEmit` know nothing of wind): wind bends smoke that already exists — which is exactly the density the guards' sight-line probe marches (`game.ts probe.querySegment`), so the AI needed nothing. |
| **motes** (`P_MOTE` = 5, `game/motes.ts`) | A sixth particle kind: no weight to speak of (settles at 4 mm/s), follows the air at 3 /s + its own meander (a cheap curl-ish sin noise, `moteMeander` 0.05 m/s), never collides or sleeps, 1.8 mm (far sub-pixel: the splat widens it to its 1.5 px floor and dims it by as much), lit by the **shadow-tested direct light alone** (`particleLight(…, credit 0)`: no dice ambient, no untraced credit) × gain 2.5 × its own slow twinkle, splatted ADDITIVELY as a dot faded in and out of its life — so outside a beam it is a black additive dot, i.e. nothing: invisible outside the light by construction. `Motes.update` tops the live estimate (a FIFO of expiries) up to `settings.moteCap` (400 / 300 / 200 / 120 / 60 per tier) inside the strong cones near the look-at point: the guards' torches and Sam's rail light (kind 1, intensity ≥ 20) within 10 m, the three nearest, each cut where `col.raycast` says the beam meets a wall, ≤ 6 births a frame per cone in two small records at hashed depths ∝ d down the beam; by day, points round the look-at with a clear `segmentBlocked` line to the sun (through the glazing, as sight goes). No strong cone near = nothing born and the rest fade inside their 6 s. Deterministic (a hash off its own serial); headless a no-op (no Particles). |
| **moths** (`game/motes.ts`) | ≤ 12 CPU boxes (a moth must orbit a point, which a particle cannot): 3.2 × 1.2 cm, `Dynamic | NoShadow`, keyed `MKEY.test + 0x4000 + i` for the motion vectors, dealt round the exterior spot lights hung over 3.5 m (the office's three sodium heads; a moth whose lamp holds nothing for it migrates, unseen, to the lamp with places unfilled — a dark lamp hoards nobody). Each lamp's pull = lit (target factor > 0.05, not broken) × night × (1 − smoothstep(3, 6, |windAt(head)|)) × (1 − smoothstep(0.75, 0.92, rain)), eased over 2.5 s up / 1.2 s down; each lamp's QUOTA is its share of the dozen by pull, ≤ 7 (two lit lamps hold six each; a third lit later takes four off them — the surplus scatter and migrate unseen). A moth's place is analytic in time: round the head at 2.2‥4.6 rad/s (either way), radius 0.14‥0.5 m breathing, bobbing 0.25 ± 0.16 m under the lens, a half-second dart in at the lens every 4‥10 s, wings a-flutter as a ± 0.9 rad roll of the box at 9 Hz (`Box.rot`); scattering = out along its own bearing three metres and down as its `shown` eases to 0, then not drawn. |
| **fixture deaths** (`game/particleFx.ts`, one call each at the existing sites) | `fixtureShatterFx` ← combat.ts's fixture-shot rule (any lit fixture but a device): pale glass + phosphor slivers raining off the fixture's whole face (34 off a panel, 18 off a bulb), a sinking puff of the tube's powder, the ballast arcing (16 sparks) — under the flash and flicker-out that were there. `breakerSparkFx` ← the breaker shot: 70 fast sparks + 25 heavy dripping ones out of the panel's face, a cough of soot. `lockSplinterFx` ← game.ts `onDoorEvent 'kick'` when the leaf's `lockBroken`: 22 long pale splinters in with the leaf off the torn keep, 6 back past the boot, the strike's 4 screws — beside `kickDustFx`'s plaster. Counts in `PFX`; all `gpuFx`-gated (headless no-ops). |
| **knobs / hooks** | `tune.wind` (0‥1, default 1), `tune.motes`, `tune.moths` + `WIND` {vents, ventK, storm, stormK, doors, doorK, gusts, gustAmp, smokeRelax, sheetWake, sheetKick, debrisK, moteMeander, …} persisted `twopointsix.wind` (Tab ▸ Weather ▸ wind / motes / moths rows + still air / full / log sources / air at cursor / reset); `__wind` {on, at(p), sources(), active(), describe(), knobs, field, stats, set({}), save, defaults, summary(), jet, storm, motes, setMotes, setMoths}; `settings.moteCap`; `tools/qa/wind-scenes.js` (`__ws.plume / paper / door / motes / moths / tube / lock / breaker / sight / cost / validate`). |
| **bounds** | ≤ 8 wind records to either consumer a frame (constants); the particles' share of the sixteen impulse slots is what the impulses leave; the solver's block is one 560-B uniform whatever the domain count; O(6 vents + 3 doors) CPU a frame; motes ≤ the tier's cap, ≤ 6 births a frame a cone, ≤ 3 cones; moths ≤ 12 boxes; no allocation on any path (fixed source objects, the record pools, the uniform); wind never touches a smoke domain's life; deterministic (clock hashes, the motes' serial; `wind-sources` proves a seeded game draws the same next Math.random with wind / motes / moths on or off). |

## Caveats / not done

- The analytic field knows nothing of furniture: a jet blows through a desk (the particles ON the desk hand their own surface for the wall-jet turn, so
  what you see on desks is right; the smoke solver's own obstacle bake and projection handle desks for the smoke). A partition standing in a door
  gust does not shadow it for the particles.
- The vents' floor is y = 0 (`floorY` on the source): the level format's `floor` is carried but v1 has one floor — TODO(G2) with the mezzanine.
- The grille is a plain box per vent, like a door's frame boxes: move a vent in the editor and move its grille with it (the editor lane can regenerate
  it from the record the way it may regenerate frames). The editor has no vent tool yet (`__ed.vent` would be a copy of `__ed.device`'s laser).
- The particles get the jets NEAR THE VIEW (≤ 8, nearest first): a papered desk trembling off screen is nobody's loss; the solver gets them ALL (culled
  per domain), so smoke off screen — which a guard may be looking through — bends regardless. With more than 8 sources over live domains at once the
  solver keeps the first 8 in roster order (vents before the storm before doors) — the office has 10 sources in all and never that many plumes.
- Motes are lit by every light's shadow ray like the chips, so a mote in the torch beam that is ALSO under a lit ceiling panel is a little brighter than
  the beam alone would make it — fine; and by construction there are none where there is no strong cone, so a lit room without a torch has no dust in
  its panel light (a choice: the cap is spent where the beams are).
- Moths are 3 cm boxes: from the play camera's ten metres they are one or two pixels crossing the bright lens and the pool — the flicker reads, the
  moth does not; the close view (`__ws.moths({ close: true })`) is where they are moths. They cast no shadow (NoShadow: twelve fluttering occluders
  20 cm under a 0.18 m emitter would only feed the denoiser noise).
- The lock's splinters fire on every kick that breaks a keep (`Door.lockBroken` goes true on any non-steel kick, locked or not — the game only kicks
  locked doors, the drills kick their staged ones): a kicked-open unlocked door still tears its latch keep, which is fair.
- Not wired to the AI on purpose: nobody remarks on a draught, the moths, or the dust; the vents make no sound (an HVAC bed under the room tone would be
  the audio lane's).
