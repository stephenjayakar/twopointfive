What the frame costs, why it swings, and the plan — rewritten after the field recordings, which promoted volumetrics to a first-class problem and caught a resolution ladder that never actually cut.
2026-08-10 · M4 Max (Chrome / Metal, dpr 2) · controlled runs on main @ 1af5479 (serialized frames, two baselines within 0.3 ms) + four F9 field sessions on main @ d5e909f (ultra / high / medium / verylow, 103–235 s each, GPU-bound, no contention) · raw: docs/perf/*.json, tools/qa/perf-report.ts

TL;DR. In controlled conditions the renderer is predictable: at high, Σ compute ≈ 3.4 ms + 8.5 ms per megapixel (14–19 ms across five views at 1777×1000); medium 11–17; ultra 22–29. Nothing merged this week regressed a pass — the four experiments cost ~2–3 ms together. In the field, the same build swings 2–4× frame to frame, and the recordings say exactly why: (1) volumetrics — median 2–3 ms but p95 6–9 and worst 13–34 whenever weapon lights, smoke and rain meet, the largest variance of any pass at every preset; (2) direct lighting — 5–12 ms means but 15–32 in a firefight, because its cost scales with lights in view (0.27 ms per light); (3) cascade re-bake bursts of 7–13 ms on camera rotation; (4) a +6 ms frame after every settings/scale change; and (5) an adaptive-resolution ladder that announced 63 cuts in one session and made none (a bug from the same afternoon — fixed, with a regression check). The renderer's structure is sound — rasterised G-buffer, analytic grid tracer, cascade cache, split signals — but three passes have cost that is unbounded by construction (direct ∝ lights, volumetrics ∝ lights × media, softblur ∝ penumbra area) and every cache bursts instead of amortizing. v3 keeps the structure and replaces exactly those pieces with fixed-cost ones: ReSTIR DI, a froxel volumetric pass, one shared denoiser, amortized caches, TAA upscaling. Phase 0 is pure wins with no look change and can start now.

1 · The renderer today, measured under control

Primary visibility is rasterised (yawed boxes + GPU-skinned mannequins → G-buffer). Everything else is software ray tracing on compute over an analytic scene — the 1 m XZ grid over ~925 static + ~300 dynamic boxes with exact empty-run / height-cull / slab fast paths, capsules for characters (a mesh BVH for their shadows on ultra), a 22-pane glass table, decal cell lists. Three stochastic signals, each with its own denoiser: direct (score all lights unshadowed → per-class top-K leaders get stratified emitter shadow rays + one importance-picked tail ray → ratio-space temporal + softblur), indirect (radiance-cascade cache c0‥c3 + dice → a ⅓-res per-pixel final gather one bounce away → firefly clamp + temporal + à-trous; bounce cards for carried torches), specular (gloss trace → spec history + à-trous; the rtreflect experiment replaces it). Then volumetrics (haze + smoke ray-march with per-step shadow rays; rain analytic), composite (glass, wet coats, decal gloss-keep), bloom, AgX, FXAA, and renderScale under an internal-height cap.

3 · Diagnosis in one list

  1. Three passes have cost that is unbounded by construction — direct ∝ lights in view (0.27 ms/light; 15–32 ms in a firefight), volumetrics ∝ lit lights × participating media along the ray (worst 13–34 ms), softblur ∝ penumbra area (bounded in practice here, but the tile skip retires ~0 % of tiles on this level so it never gets its discount). Everything else scales cleanly with pixels or is small.
  2. Every cache bursts instead of amortizing — cascade probes re-bake in 7–13 ms lumps on rotation; a scale/setting change re-bakes everything and resets every history (+6 ms frame).
  3. The controller was blind — the ladder tolerated 29 fps by design until today, then could not act because of the bug above, and the HUD's per-pass numbers lied twice (dormant slots). With the recorder + report we now have a trustworthy loop: F9 → dump → tables.
  4. The features are not the problem — glass, decals, particles, weather, wet, bounce cards ≈ 2–3 ms all-in and flat; RT reflections would add ≤ 2 ms replacing gloss.
  5. Ultra is honest now — 22–29 ms calm, 31 mean / 46 p95 in a stress session at 1959×1200 ×0.70; with a working ladder it would have settled near ×0.55 and ~45 fps.

4 · Renderer v3 — principles (unchanged in spirit, re-weighted by the field)

  • Fixed work per pixel (or per froxel), chosen well — direct = 1 shadow ray via reservoir resampling regardless of light count; volumetrics = a froxel volume with a capped per-froxel light list and temporal reuse instead of per-pixel marches through every lit light; reflections = 1 GGX ray on ≤0.45-rough pixels at half res; the gather keeps its rays into the cache.
  • One denoiser core, three clients — shared reprojection (surface + virtual hit point), variance, history clamp, edge-stopped à-trous; shadow / diffuse / specular parameterisations. Retires ratio-space softblur+dtemporal, the gather's private temporal+à-trous, the spec chain.
  • Amortize every cache; never burst — cascades, reach masks, decal cell lists, bounce cards get fixed per-frame budgets with priority queues; camera motion and edits are incremental by construction.
  • No invalidation hitches — histories survive resize and setting flips (rescale/reproject), pipelines pre-warm at boot, scale changes are rare and hysteretic (the ladder now holds 50–60 with real cuts).
  • Temporal upscaling in front of the display — TAA with upsampling replaces FXAA + raw scale; internal 60–70 % by default with better edges, faster convergence for every stochastic signal, invisible dynamic-resolution steps.
  • Keep the good parts untouched — the analytic grid tracer (no hardware RT in WebGPU; this is our BVH and it is exact and cheap), the rasterised G-buffer, the radiance-cascade cache as the GI terminator, the post chain, and the one-model rule (the light meter / guards' vision read the same lighting — v3 keeps the probe pass bit-compatible).
  • One principled BRDF (base colour / metalness / roughness; GGX + Smith + Schlick, VNDF-sampled) at primary, gather and reflection hits — rtreflect already plumbed rough/metal per box.
  • Budgets are tests — the pane harness becomes tools/qa/perf-bench with per-pass budgets asserted per preset; the F9 recorder + perf-report stay as the field check; both run after every renderer merge.

5 · Phases — each ships, each is A/B-able against today, each has a field recording as its exit test

PHASE 0
Budget discipline and the burst/hitch fixes — no look change, start now. (a) Amortized cascade upkeep: a fixed probe budget per frame with a priority queue (newly visible, stale, near the player first) → kills the 7–13 ms bursts. (b) Histories that survive resize and setting flips + boot-time pipeline pre-warm → kills the +6 ms frames and the first-search hitch. (c) Volumetrics stop-gap before the froxel rewrite: cap the lights marched per step to the k strongest by unshadowed contribution at the sample (k=4), early-out on transmittance, adaptive step count by segment length — bounds the worst case now. (d) Particle births capped per frame; smoke domains beyond N run at half rate. (e) renderScale re-expressed relative to the cap (no dead zone) — the ladder fix itself already shipped. (f) perf-bench with asserted budgets; world.upload incremental (1 ms CPU). Exit test: repeat the four recordings → σ under 40 % of today's, worst frame under mean × 1.6, ladder cuts announced = made.
PHASE 1
TAAU replacing FXAA + raw scale. Jittered projection, display-space history, neighbourhood clip, reactive mask for particles/rain/muzzle flashes, sharpen; presets default to internal 0.6–0.7. Deletes FXAA. Risk: ghosting on thin box edges and mannequins. Exit: crops of thin geometry under motion, gather convergence frames, cost ≈ 0.6–1 ms, a recording at high showing p5 fps up with no new worst frames.
PHASE 2a
ReSTIR DI for direct lighting (exp/restir-di). Per-pixel reservoirs, 2–4 candidates from the existing unshadowed scoring (incl. today's analytic emitter / area sampling as the target pdf), temporal reuse by reprojection, one spatial pass, 1 shadow ray to the survivor (2 on ultra), MIS weights that keep shadow boundaries unbiased; transient lights (muzzle, lightning) injected as fresh candidates. Deletes the leader/tail selection and per-class K; keeps tile culling as the candidate source. Risk: medium (correlation artefacts, boundary bias); the light meter must read the same irradiance. Exit: equal-time noise vs today at 37 lights and at 6, cost flat vs light count, penumbra crops ×3, a loud recording with direct p95 ≤ 4 ms.
PHASE 2b
Froxel volumetrics. A camera-aligned froxel grid (e.g. 160×90×64) accumulating in-scattering per froxel from a per-froxel capped light list (culled by the same tile lists + reach masks) with shadow terms sampled once per froxel and reused temporally (reprojected 3D history), then a single front-to-back integration per pixel; smoke density and rain feed the same volume. Cost becomes ∝ froxels × k lights, independent of pixels and of how many beams cross a ray. Deletes the per-pixel march. Risk: medium — beam edges soften at froxel resolution (jitter + temporal fixes most), thin laser beams stay analytic. Exit: the QRF-in-smoke recording with volumetrics p95 ≤ 1.5 ms and beams that still read in crops.
PHASE 3
The shared denoiser (SVGF/ReLAX-class core) fed by the ReSTIR shadow signal, the gather's diffuse signal and the reflection signal; retire softblur/dtemporal, the gather's temporal + à-trous, the spec chain. Deletes three bespoke filters. Risk: the ratio-space trick that keeps transients history-free must survive as a mode. Exit: temporal-sd tiles under a walking torch, ghost trails on strafing guards, fixed cost per pixel.
PHASE 4
Materials and detail everywhere — the principled BRDF at all three hit types; rtreflect merged onto the shared denoiser; the high-frequency detail prototype (albedo / normal / roughness / edge wear at the primary hit) feeding roughness into reflections. Taste, not tech — pages first, as agreed.
PHASE 5
Ultra only, optional — ReSTIR GI on top of the cascade cache, mesh closest-hit for characters in reflections/GI, 2-ray reflections.
