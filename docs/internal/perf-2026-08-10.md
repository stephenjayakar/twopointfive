## A. Analytic torch bounce — "torch bounce cards"  · `settings.torchBounceCards` · frame flag 33554432 · KEPT, default off, presets: see A.6

### A.1 What was built

`src/render/shaders/cards.wgsl` (new, one workgroup of 64 per nominated light, dispatched inside the `gather` pass so its cost is timed with it),
`fgather.wgsl` (staging, `cardIrradiance`, the exclusion in `directAtPoint` and the ray loop), `common.wgsl` (`onCard`, the frame block's
`cardCfg` / `cardSlots`, the flag), `frame.ts` / `engine.ts` / `lights.ts` (`RtLight.card`, `slot`, `cardSlotsFor`, FRAME_BYTES 416 → 464),
`passes.ts` (an 8×4 rgba32f `cards` texture — a texture, not an 11th storage buffer: the scene group already sits at Chrome/Metal's 10-per-stage
budget), `rigProps.ts` (the guards' torches / weapon lights and Sam's rail light are `card` lights), Tab ▸ Indirect light toggle + count.

Per frame, for each nominated light (uploaded, steady, kind 1, `card`; the 8 nearest the look-at point if more):
1. **Lane 0** traces the beam axis from the lens (the carrier's boxes / capsules skipped — the rule every shadow ray toward that light already
   uses) to the first surface within 6 m: centre **C** on the surface, normal **n**, distance t. No card on a miss, a character (capsule), or grazing
   incidence (cos < 0.25). Claim radius **R** = min(1 m, 1.15 · t · tan(outer) / cos(incidence)).
2. **64 lanes** trace one direction each, stratified uniformly in solid angle over the outer cone, and keep the samples whose hit lies in the
   CLAIM REGION W = { x : |(x − C)·n| < 2.5 cm, normal(x)·n > 0.8, in-plane distance < R } (`onCard`, the one predicate both shaders use).
3. Rows written: (C, R) · (n, slot) · (Φ_L, r_q) · (C_q, 1) — Φ_L = radiance × area of the lit patch per channel, C_q / r_q = the flux-weighted
   centroid and RMS radius of the kept samples (a spot's profile is centre-heavy; a beam half over a wall's edge is lopsided).

The gather, per half-res (third-res at high) pixel, AFTER its four cosine rays (their `rnd()` sequence is untouched, so the ray estimate is value
for value what it was): per live card, the patch as four sub-emitters at radius r_q round C_q, each Φ_L/4 · cosθ_e · cosθ_r / (r² + r_q²/2)
(one-sided emitter cosine, receiver cosine, the on-axis-exact disc form — no 1/max(d², R²) kink up close); one card picked ∝ its unshadowed
luminance; ONE shadow ray toward a point on that card's equivalent disc (radius √2·r_q), the point drawn from a per-pixel R2 sequence stepped by
the frame index (so consecutive frames cover the disc evenly and the history's running mean settles on the fractional visibility); smoke
transmittance on that segment when smoke is up; under 0.002 W/m² total the term is credited unshadowed (no ray). And the EXCLUSION: a cosine ray
whose hit lies in W_k drops light k's direct term at that hit — on the on-screen path by subtracting exactly what the direct pass delivered there
(evalLight × its own shadow ray with the direct pass's skip rule × smoke), on the off-screen path by leaving it out of the one-pick sum.

### A.2 Why the energy is right (derivation; `tools/qa/perf-probes.ts cards` holds every step numerically)

- For a point source of intensity I(ω) = c·I₀·f(cos α), f = smoothstep(cosO, cosI, ·), the power through dω is I dω whatever it lands on
  (E = I cosθ/d², dA = d² dω / cosθ ⇒ E dA = I dω). So the flux the direct pass deposits inside W is Φ_W = Σ_{samples in W} c·I₀·f(cos α_j)·
  window(d_j)·ΔΩ (× smoke transmittance), ΔΩ = 2π(1 − cosO)/64 — the same factors `evalLight` applies (its 1/max(d², r²) is 1/d² here). Closed
  form when the whole cone lands: ∫ I dω = π·I₀·(2 − cosI − cosO) (smoothstep integrates to half its ramp): 13.620 W for the 70 W/sr 7°/19° torch;
  the 64 stratified samples give 13.620 W (−0.00 %); brute-force integration of the profile over the wall gives 13.620 W.
- A diffuse surface turns irradiance E into radiosity ρE = π·L, so the patch's radiance × area is Φ_L = Σ_j ρ(x_j) ⊙ Φ_j / π — 1.821 (red channel)
  for S1 by the closed form × the wall's albedo 0.42; **the GPU card row read back 1.8209** (and r_q 0.082 m, C on the wall plane, R 0.173).
- The card's irradiance at a receiver, E = Σ_i (Φ_L/4)·cosθ_e,i·cosθ_r,i / (r_i² + r_q²/2), against brute-force integration of the true patch
  radiance over the wall (400×240 cells) at six receivers 0.6–4 m off (floor points and a box front facing the wall): −0.0 … −0.7 %, worst 0.7 %.
- Nothing is counted twice and nothing is dropped: the card claims exactly the flux whose hit passes `onCard`, and the gather excludes exactly the
  hits that pass `onCard` — for any R. Flux landing outside W (past R, on the floor under a wall spot, on a man in the beam, on a box in front)
  stays with the rays. The radiance cache (cascades), the light meter, the direct pass and the volumetrics are untouched: the card replaces only
  the gather's FIRST-bounce discovery of W; the dice term at secondary hits (second bounce onward) is what it always was.

### A.3 The energy check — and what it found about the shipped path

The brief's check ("mean indirect in a box round the spot, cards on vs off, converged, within 3 %") cannot pass, and the reason is the finding of
the day: **the shipped ray path delivers a small fraction of this bounce**, so OFF is the biased side. Measured on S1's floor tile (220×130 px in
front of the spot), linear irradiance from the pass textures, 200-frame means:

| estimator (K = giFireflyK) | mean E (× indirectScale) | vs cards |
|---|---|---|
| raw gather output, rays only, K 0, **absolute cap lifted** (temporary shader edit) — the unbiased reference | 0.0885 / 0.0921 (two runs) | — |
| raw gather output, **cards on**, K 0 | 0.0952 / 0.0956 | **+5 % ± 3 %** (the reference's own run-to-run spread is 4 %) |
| raw gather output, rays only, K 0, shipped cap 24 on hit radiance | 0.0638 | cards ×1.5 |
| filtered (temporal + à-trous) `gi[0]`, rays only, K 0 (= ultra) | 0.0141 | cards ×6.4 |
| filtered `gi[0]`, rays only, K 4 (= high‥verylow) | 0.0036 | cards ×25 |
| filtered `gi[0]`, cards on, K 0 / K 4 | 0.0898 / 0.0916 | — |

So the cards agree with the unbiased ray estimator to within its noise (the +5 % residual is inside 2σ; candidates for a real +2–3 %: the
on-screen exclusion subtracts the analytic torch term from a penumbra-FILTERED direct texture, and the filter smears a little of the spot past R
where nothing is excluded). Against what ships, the cards return the bounce the pipeline has been throwing away: the absolute cap of 24 W/m²/sr
on a gather hit's radiance removes 36 % here by itself (the patch peaks at 50.6; perf-probes prints this), the temporal pass's variance clip
(history clamped to the current 3×3's mean ± σ — a heavy-tailed estimator's rare bright samples are clipped away the frame after they land)
removes most of the rest, and K = 4 caps each ray at 4× the cache's estimate ÷ π on top. In a dark room lit by one torch on a wall, `high`
showed 4 % of the physical first bounce and `ultra` 15 %; with cards both show ~100 %, and it holds still.

**This is a look change, not only a noise change**: rooms with a torch near a wall get visibly (correctly) brighter bounce — the "flashlight
circle as an area light" the brief asked for. Crops: `docs/shots/perf_A_s1_high_K4_{off,on}_{final,indirect}.jpg`, `…K0…` (300×300 ×3).

## B. Lossless hunts

The rule was: implement behind a default-ON flag only if pixel A/B proves max diff 0 AND the timing wins ≥ 0.15 ms. Read against the code as it
stands, most of the brief's candidates are either already in the tree or cannot clear 0.15 ms because the pass they live in is smaller than that
at `high`. Nothing in this section was merged; each line says why, with the number that decided it.

| # | candidate | finding | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| b1 | volumetrics: early-out at T < 0.01 | already there (`volumetrics.wgsl` smoke march: `if (T < 0.01) { break; }`); the haze march has no extinction, so no T to cut on | exists |
| b1 | volumetrics: steps adaptive to the segment | already there: haze `steps = clamp((θB − θA)/0.11 + 3, 4, volSteps)` (equi-angular), smoke `clamp(span/(1.5·voxel) + 1, 4, 112)` | exists |
| b1 | volumetrics: skip lights by reach mask in the per-step loop | not used there today, and NOT exact: the mask is a five-look sample per cell (reach.ts header: 36 cells where a light does get in are level 0), so it would fail the max-diff-0 gate the way ReachMask itself would; and the lights it could skip are the static ones with `volumetric > 0` — desk lamp, storage bulb, three street lamps, the beacons (off outside a blackout) — the torches carry the all-ones mask. The whole pass is 0.09–0.21 ms at high (0.13 at ultra): there is no 0.15 ms in it | declined (measured pass size) |
| b2 | softblur: skip fully-lit tiles beyond SoftTileSkip | a fully lit tile IS a hint-free tile (the direct pass writes hint 0 for lit pixels; only a blocked ray writes a width), so SoftTileSkip already retires it; fully DARK tiles carry hints but hit the per-pixel `maxA < 1e-6` exact shortcut. "Zero penumbra variance in history" needs history → not lossless. **But** `passes.softTileStats()` says the lossless tile skip retires **0 %** of tiles in S3, 0 % in the lobby and 0.6 % in the cubicles view at high: since the panels' ranges grew to where they fall to nothing (26 m), every pixel has some far, walled-off or occluded dim leader whose blocked ray writes a hint, so every tile is flagged and the whole chain runs everywhere. Recovering that is lossy → C.4 | exists, but inert on this level → C.4 |
| b3 | gather: skip the second cache tap when the first weight ≈ 1 | already there: `diceIrradiance` takes 1–3 taps by the normal's squared components (`if (n2.x > 0.001)` …) — an axis-aligned surface (nearly all of them) takes exactly one | exists |
| b3 | gather: 4 → 3 rays where history is long | lossy by definition → C; not attempted (the cards remove the case that motivated it) | → C |
| b4 | direct: leaders under 1/512 of the running sum skip their ray | already there and 25× more aggressive: `if (luminance(f) < 0.02 * W[c]) { leadS += f; continue; }` credits a leader under 2 % of its class unshadowed, and the tail under 2 % rides the leaders' visibility | exists |
| b4 | direct: tighter per-tile light lists with the reach level split | the tile list culls by `lightReachesBox` (geometry, exact); culling by reach LEVEL would drop level-1/2 lights that do contribute (they are ranked down, not out) → not lossless; level 0 is per CELL and a tile spans cells → a per-tile AND of "level 0 in every cell the tile touches" is exact but saves only the scoring of walled-off lights, which `lightReachLevel` already ends after one word read | declined (no ray to save) |
| b5 | mesh shadows: skip the BVH walk when the capsule broad phase misses by > r | `meshShadowT` already gates on the bounding sphere and then the root node's box (an exact AABB of the posed mesh) before any triangle; gating on the CAPSULES needs a proven bound r with mesh ⊂ capsules ⊕ r over all poses — `mesh-shadow-ref.ts` says 6–12 % of mesh-blocked rays touch no capsule at all, so r is not small; not attempted without that bound. Mesh pass at ultra with the four men: 0.128 / 0.125 ms (cards off / on) | declined (needs a bound; pass is 0.13 ms) |
| b6 | CPU per-frame work | measured in the page over 260 frames (S3, 1181 boxes of which 280 dynamic): `world.upload` **1.28 ms / frame** (pack 0.44 + `buildGrid` 0.84 — every static box is re-packed and re-registered every frame), `lights.upload` 0.04, engine.render's CPU side 1.39 in all, the whole game step 4.1. Not on the critical path on this machine (the GPU frame is 11–17 ms and the JS runs alongside it), and the obvious fix — pack statics once behind `staticsDirty`, keep a static prefix per grid cell and append the dynamics — rewrites the one routine every exactness argument above `traceClosest` leans on. Logged, not touched in a lane that must merge clean; worth ~1 ms of main-thread time on a slow CPU | measured, deferred |

difference is temporal; the numbers above are the evidence).
